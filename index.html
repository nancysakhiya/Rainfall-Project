Rainfall Trends in India Analysis

Exploring Long-Term Trends, Seasonal Patterns, and Anomalies in Rainfall Data

Project Overview

This analysis uncovers long-term trends, seasonal patterns, and anomalies in India's rainfall data. We aim to detect years with extreme or deficient rainfall, explore relationships between seasonal and annual rainfall, and forecast future rainfall using time series models.

Dataset Overview

Our dataset contains monthly, seasonal, and annual rainfall measurements spanning over a century. These values are essential for understanding variability, detecting anomalies, and predicting future trends.
